I am a third-year PhD student at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), advised by Prof. Hao Li. My research explores 3D generative modeling and geometry learning for digital garments, including virtual try-on, garment retargeting, sewing pattern generation, and garment reconstruction.
